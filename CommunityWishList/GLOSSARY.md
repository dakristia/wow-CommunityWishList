# CommunityWishList glossary

**Wish**
One request for an item: the item, the wanted quantity, and optionally a note, a gold offer and a priority. A wish is *open* until it is filled or the wisher closes it.
_Avoid_: order, request

**Wisher**
The player who made a wish. Only the wisher changes a wish.

**Recipient**
The wisher's character that the items are mailed to. Each wish names exactly one recipient.

**Audience**
Who can see a wish: the wisher's guild, one or more groups, or both. The wisher picks it for each wish.

**Group**
A named set of up to 40 characters that share wishes with each other, independent of guilds. A character joins a group only by accepting an invite from a member, and can be in several groups at once.
_Avoid_: party (that is the in-game party), community (that is Blizzard's Communities feature)

**Creator**
The character who made a group. Only the creator renames the group or removes a member.

**Invite**
A member's offer to a character to join a group. The character becomes a member by accepting it. Any member can invite.

**Member**
A character in a group. A member is *invited*, then *joined*, and ends as *left* (by its own choice) or *removed* (by the creator). A left or removed character can be invited again.

**Claim**
A claimer's promise to send part of a wish, for example 40 of 200. A claim is *open*, then *sent*, then *received*. An open claim can instead end as *cancelled* by the claimer or *released* by the wisher.
_Avoid_: order, reservation

**Claimer**
The character who made a claim and mails the items.

**Sent**
The claimer has mailed the claimed items. The sent quantity of all claims counts toward filling the wish.

**Received**
The wisher has taken the claimer's mail with the item out of the mailbox.

**Filled**
A wish whose sent quantity has reached its wanted quantity. A filled wish closes by itself.

**Player**
A set of characters that one person has linked as alts. A character that is not linked counts as a player on its own.

**Cache**
Every wish and claim this account has seen, from any member. The cache lets members see and claim wishes of members who are offline.

**Relay**
Passing a cached wish on to a member who did not have it, so it spreads without its wisher being online.
